# Family F preregistration summary

Family F treats the first F05 signal of a directional event as a non-trading probe. A later same-direction signal can be admitted only after finite directional confirmation, within a finite event age and finite event trade budget.

The finite grid contains 54 specifications:

- confirmation distance: 5, 10 or 15 pips;
- maximum event age: 120, 240 or 480 minutes;
- maximum accepted F05 trades per event: 1, 2 or 3;
- same-time same-direction B02 confirmation override: disabled or enabled.

B02 and all accepted exits remain unchanged. Blocked F05 signals remain in event memory as observations. At most one H1 finalist may access H2. No Family F candidate may access 2025 before exact 2024 MT4 parity and a separate authorization.
