# USDJPY-HYP-039 Suppression Lineage Clarification v2

## Finding

The exact 500-trade HYP-037 Short authority was not generated by applying active suppression to Short raw signals alone.

HYP-036 first processed the chronological union of Long and Short raw Pullback signals with one shared same-variant lifecycle. It accepted 1,332 events: 832 Long and 500 Short. HYP-037 then selected the 500 accepted Short rows without changing their inherited chronology.

Therefore HYP-039 remains a **Short-only trading candidate**, while the suppression state must observe accepted raw events of both sides. Accepted Long events occupy the variant through signal index `i+17` but must never place an HYP-039 order or contribute HYP-039 P/L.

## Decisive event

On 2024-10-31:

- Long event at 02:45 UTC, signal index 45,750, was accepted.
- Its shared variant lifecycle remained active through index 45,767.
- The Short raw signal at 06:30 UTC was therefore suppressed.
- The next accepted Short was 09:30 UTC, index 45,777.

A Short-only suppression implementation instead accepted 06:30 and suppressed 09:30. That produced 499/500 common rows even though all common row fields, chronology and currency matched.

## Scientific boundary

This clarification:

- does not change HYP-037 or HYP-038 decisions;
