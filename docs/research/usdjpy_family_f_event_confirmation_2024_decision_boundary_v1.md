# Family F decision boundary

The Family F search is a single finite attempt under the current 2024 architecture diagnosis.

- No H1 finalist: close Family F without H2 access and activate the 2023 fast M1-bar fallback.
- H1 finalist but H2 failure: close Family F and activate the 2023 fast M1-bar fallback.
- H1 and H2 pass: freeze one exact candidate, implement it in Core, complete 2024 MT4 parity, and request separate authorization before any renewed 2025 H1 execution.

No grid expansion or post-result threshold adjustment is authorized inside Family F.
