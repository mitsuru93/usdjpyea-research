# USDJPY-HYP-041 / F05 2025H1 Loss Recovery and Portable Architecture Study

## Objective

Preserve the material 2023–2024 F05 expectancy while improving the known 2025H1 F05 loss of `-¥13,844` and the B02＋F05 portfolio loss of `-¥20,808` under exact chronology, JPY accounting, full-equity and margin contracts.

This is an independent F05 v2 study. It does not reopen or change the formal decisions of HYP-027, HYP-029 or HYP-032. It also does not modify HYP-039 or HYP-040.

## Period roles

- 2020–2022: optional historical reference or auxiliary analysis; not a binding gate.
- 2023–2024: research and candidate-construction period.
- 2025H1: validation period. Known baseline results and repeated validation do not reclassify this period.
- 2025H2: not automatically used or substituted.

## First binding stage

Before any candidate outcome is calculated, rebuild 2023–2024 and 2025H1 F05 trades in a common schema and attribute losses to seven exclusive primary buckets:

1. Entry immediately adverse.
2. Temporary profit then loss.
3. Edge decay during extended hold.
4. Same-session consecutive loss.
5. Market-state misfit.
6. Chronology or same-timestamp interaction.
7. Other.

Each bucket must report trades, net, gross loss, average and maximum loss, loss share, difference from 2023–2024 and B02 overlap.

## Finite candidate boundary

At most three single candidates may be frozen:

- C1: chronology-aware loss-persistence permission.
- C2: one F05-specific market-state risk-compression mechanism.
- C3: continuation-failure or giveback lifecycle.

One combined candidate is permitted only when the single-candidate ledgers establish distinct affected trades and additive economics before combined 2025H1 access. Total candidates may not exceed four. Only one selected candidate may proceed to Core and MT4.

## Prohibited paths

No unlimited parameter search, result-seeking grid, post-hoc side/session/month removal, exact HYP-027 threshold reuse, exact S3 reuse, fixed SL/TP/hold grid, dependency on unmerged parallel code, HYP-039/HYP-040 modification, automatic 2025H2 substitution, production or live authorization.

## Decisions

- `PASS_F05_V2_2025H1_RECOVERY`
- `PASS_F05_V2_PARTIAL_RECOVERY`
- `FAIL_F05_V2_PORTABLE_RECOVERY`
- irreparable `TECHNICAL_NO_RESULT`

The study does not stop at decomposition: after the finite catalog is frozen, it proceeds through 2023–2024 Research, maximum-one candidate selection, Core/MT4 parity, 2025H1 validation, portfolio/full-equity/margin evaluation and final closure.
