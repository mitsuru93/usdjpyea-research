# USDJPY Post-Invalidation Trajectory Diagnosis v1

Decision: **do not preregister another F05 exit family; move to B02 loss-structure diagnosis**

## Scope

The six closed Family H trigger populations were compared across 2023, 2024 H1 and 2024 H2 using only features known by the second checkpoint:

- structural change from the first to second checkpoint;
- recovery from the interval minimum;
- giveback from the interval maximum;
- endpoint position within the observed range;
- structural depth and pre-entry features.

Eleven fixed, mechanism-interpretable partitions were checked. No 2025 evidence was accessed.

## Only cross-period-positive partition

For both 30/60-minute parent cells, trades that had recovered at least 20 pips from the interval minimum by 60 minutes produced a positive early-exit effect in every development period:

- 2023: JPY +1,077
- 2024 H1: JPY +1,115
- 2024 H2: JPY +534
- pooled: JPY +2,726

This describes a failed rebound: price recovered materially from its trough but still had not reaccepted the original breakout level.

## Why no successor is registered

Applying the entire 2023 effect would move the portfolio only from JPY -9,279 to JPY -8,202. The mandatory positive-total-net gate remains unreachable.

Every other fixed trajectory partition failed to produce a positive exit effect in all three periods. Adding more rebound thresholds to Family H would therefore be result-driven subdivision, not a plausible route to the portfolio objective.

## Portfolio-level implication

Under the historical 2024-compatible 2023 lineage:

- B02: JPY -12,459
- F05: JPY +3,180
- portfolio: JPY -9,279

F05 exit refinement cannot solve a deficit primarily generated by B02. Research must move from F05 exit timing to B02 entry-quality and regime structure.

## Retained findings

- Failed rebound is a real trajectory distinction.
- Its economic magnitude is too small for the 2023 portfolio gate.
- B02 is the binding 2023 architecture problem.

## Prohibited reuse

- Do not create a rebound-threshold repair of Family H.
- Do not repeatedly partition F05 exit populations to search for a positive result.
- Do not use 2025 to choose a trajectory threshold.
